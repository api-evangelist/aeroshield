# AeroShield

> AeroShield (AeroShield Materials, Inc.) is a Waltham, Massachusetts materials science company spun
> out of MIT in 2019. It manufactures the world's most transparent silica aerogel, laminated into
> insulated glass units so a double-pane window or door outperforms a triple pane while staying
> thinner and lighter. It sells business-to-business to window, door and skylight manufacturers.
